Irish flashcards

Add a card

Vocabulary (single words): put the Irish word on the front, with an or na if you like, and the English on the back. Listen finds the word without the article. Phrase: the Irish phrase on the front and the English on the back. Hear it copies the front into abair.ie. Conjugation: put only the Irish forms on the back, one per line.

Add many at once

One card per line: front | back | note (the note is optional). Each back must fit on one line. For a multi-line back, use Add a card above.

Backup and transfer

Cards are saved in this browser only. Export to back them up or move them to another device.
